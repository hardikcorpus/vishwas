About Us

An experienced partner in specialty materials.

Vishwas Enterprises is an industrial materials distributor with 25 years of experience serving customers across North India. We represent MOLYKOTE® specialty lubricants by DuPont and DOWSIL™ silicone products by Dow.

Managing Director · Vishwas Enterprises

His love for the automotive industry and the products we supply led him to build a distribution channel of his own.
